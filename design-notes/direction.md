# Recur — concept and direction

## Brief
- Product: subscriptions tracker. Theme: light with a dark switch. Device: desktop web first, works at 390.
- Deliverables: a designed concept page, a self-playing product demo and a single-screen clickable app, for the same product.
- "Modern, classy, premium, and high-budget." Built from an empty folder: nothing reused from earlier projects.
- Built with a personal design toolkit: demo kit, interaction recipes, check scripts.
- Delivery: one self-contained HTML page plus the source.
- Direction: decided and built through; recorded here and shown in the work.

## Concept
**Recur shows every subscription on one month dial, warns you before a price changes, and cancels with one hold.**

Job: When a charge I'd forgotten about lands, I want to see every renewal coming before it happens, so I can keep only
what I use without digging through emails and account menus.

- Who: a working professional with 8–15 subscriptions, checking on a laptop on a Sunday evening, about once a week.
- Outcome: they catch a price rise or an unused plan before it charges, and act on it in under a minute.
- Surface: Operate (the app); Persuade (the concept page); the demo sits in a landing-hero setting.

## Five lines
| Line | |
|---|---|
| Product | A subscriptions tracker for one person: every recurring charge, what's coming, what changed |
| Character | Composed, exact, warm |
| World | A watch face and a desk calendar: the month as a dial, the day as a hand |
| Moments | The month dial (summary); the cancel hold and the saving that lands after it |
| Signature | The month dial: each renewal a mark at its day, length by cost, colour by category; a brass hand at today |

Idea sentence: on Recur, **the month's renewal dates** become **a watch dial whose hand you can see coming**.

Template test: a budgeting app would show a pie of spend by category. Only a renewals product has dated charges
that arrive in order, so the dial belongs to this product.

## Direction contract
| Block | Contents |
|---|---|
| Feeling | Composed, exact, warm |
| Direction and why | "Instrument on porcelain": one midnight dial object on a quiet warm-white workspace. It wins because the product's real data (dated renewals) gives the hero its form, and one deep object is how warmth reads as premium |
| Surface | Operate (app), Persuade (concept page) |
| Scene | A person at home on a Sunday evening under lamp light, laptop on the table, checking what will leave their account → light by default; dark switch asked for |
| Type | Newsreader (display figures, concept headlines, 400/500, optical size on) + Hanken Grotesk (all UI, 400/500/600; tabular figures with a narrow decimal point. Schibsted Grotesk was tried first and dropped: its tnum widens the "." to a full digit, "$15 .49"). Scale 12/13/14/16/18/22/28/40/56/72 |
| Colour | Ground porcelain oklch(0.975 0.004 80); ink oklch(0.22 0.012 260); dial midnight oklch(0.25 0.035 255); brass accent = today / coming this week; categories: streaming coral (30), reading olive (120), music teal (195), software blue (255), fitness rose (350); green = money saved; red = price rise. Restrained posture, brass ≤ 5% |
| Density and grid | 12 columns, contained at 1280, 24 px gutters; dial card 7 cols, "Worth a look" 5 cols, table full width |
| Radius and depth | 20 px cards, 12 px inner, 999 px pills; soft neutral shadow on the dial object only, hairlines elsewhere |
| Motion | Quiet and exact: digits roll on changes, the cancelled mark leaves the dial, the hold fill sweeps. Nothing loops |
| Signature | The month dial, top left of the app and the first thing on the concept page |
| References | None used (built without references). Quality bar from the taste profile: Apple, Stripe, Linear, Mercury qualities only |

## Candidates considered (internal, short)
1. Instrument on porcelain (picked).
2. Statement ledger: the month as a bank-statement column with a running balance; strong but reads like a bank.
3. Receipt stack: each subscription as a printed slip; memorable but overdone for a weekly tool.
